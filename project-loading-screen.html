// Overview

A concept art series exploring the aesthetics of video game loading screens as a design medium in their own right. Rather than treating the loading screen as a functional placeholder, this project reimagines it as a cinematic moment — a brief, immersive window into a world.

Each piece was built in Blender, focusing on environment construction, atmospheric lighting, and typographic composition. The goal was to create spaces that feel inhabited and lived-in, even within the constraints of a single static frame.

After Effects was used for post-production — adding depth through colour grading, subtle motion blur, and light leaks that give each render a filmic, tactile quality.
